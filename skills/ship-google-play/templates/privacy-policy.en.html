Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information on servers of our own: the App has no account system and no server. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions, app performance and diagnostics (for example crash data).

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the App's settings. On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in the device's Google settings.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the App uses Google Play Games Services to record your achievements and leaderboard scores. Your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and scores may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings. This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy; we do not receive your email address or store this data ourselves.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation needed to unlock what you bought; we never see your payment details.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Data handled by the third-party services above is transmitted with encryption in transit (HTTPS). We do not retain personal data ourselves.
